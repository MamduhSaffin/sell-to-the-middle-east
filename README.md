# GCC Market Entry

Independent, free and multilingual reference website for Malaysian businesses exploring Saudi Arabia, the UAE and wider GCC market-entry pathways.

Live site: https://gccmarketentry.me

## Purpose

GCC Market Entry is an educational and planning resource. It helps businesses understand market-entry questions before they commit inventory, compliance spending, platform fees or advisory costs.

Current areas include:
- GCC market-entry roadmap
- Saudi Arabia and UAE country guides
- Product/category guidance
- Routes-to-market comparison
- Official-source library
- Malaysia Export Desk
- Free cost planner
- Readiness checker
- Arabic localisation and market-testing guides

## Independence

The website is not operated by, endorsed by or dependent on any marketplace, employer, certification body or government authority. Commercial and regulatory claims should be verified with the relevant official source.

The resource is intentionally separate from **TGPU Gulf Advisory & Trade**. Users who later want optional human support can explore TGPU Gulf separately, while GCC Market Entry remains a free knowledge resource.
